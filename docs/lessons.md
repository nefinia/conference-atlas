# What the first conference showed

Conference Atlas was first used at a conference of about 330 people in October 2026. It was built in about five days before the event and updated during the week. These numbers come from the organisers' estimates and from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which samples, so they are approximate.

## Use

- About 330 people registered and 240 to 260 were present on the busiest days.
- About 2,700 to 2,900 visits in the week, around 10 per attendee. Monday was the peak. Use dropped after Wednesday.
- Four in ten visits came through the organisers' email. The rest were people reopening the app from their phone, links shared in messaging apps and QR codes.
- 87 percent on phones. A third of the visits were between 08:00 and 12:00.
- Sync reached 21 devices, the assistant about 15 people and the board 16 people. Each is about 6 to 8 percent of the people present.
- The assistant cost 0.08 dollars for the week.

## What it means for the template

1. The programme on a phone carries the value. The clock in local time, the stars and the overlap warning come first.
2. Distribution decides use. The organiser kit starts with the email paragraph, the slide and the QR code.
3. The first morning sets the habit. Have an announcement ready on day one.
4. Social features find a small group. A public board gathered posts and no conversation. In the template they are switches.
5. Cost is no reason to leave the assistant out. The budget cap stays.
6. New announcements did not appear in the first version because of caching. The service worker now asks the network first for the page, `conference.js` and `live.json`.
7. Feedback comes after the event and only when asked, so the last screen has a one-tap rating.
8. Counting started after the conference, so stars and notes of that week were not counted. The template counts from the start, with hourly totals, no cookies and no identifiers. A local mark tells new devices from returning ones.
